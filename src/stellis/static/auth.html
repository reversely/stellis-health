How Stellis runs

Stellis runs on its own server on Nebius AI Cloud. Your records are encrypted at rest and zero-access: Stellis can't read what's stored. AI models are served on Nebius Token Factory with zero data retention and a privacy proxy. Stellis conducts research with Nemotron Super 120B combined with Tavily, chosen for its accuracy on our evaluations with synthetic patient interactions.

Your deviceWeb or phone

  • Your camera video never leaves your device

Stellis serverNebius AI Cloud, United States

  • Your records are encrypted (AES-256-GCM)
  • Only your password unlocks them, so Stellis can't read what's stored
  • Visit recordings are transcribed here, not sent elsewhere
  • Your name and ID numbers are removed before anything goes to the AI model

Nebius Token FactoryNemotron Super 120B

  • Stores nothing Stellis sends it

Web researchMedlinePlus, PubMed, Tavily

  • When Stellis looks something up for you, it sends only the topic, never your name or details
